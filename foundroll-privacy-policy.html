FOUNDROLL CAMERA

Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Your photos stay on your device

FoundRoll processes photos on your iPhone. Photos you capture or choose from your library are not uploaded to FoundRoll servers. FoundRoll does not operate a photo-storage service.

Permissions

  • Camera access is used only to take photos.
  • Photos access is used only for a photo you choose and to save the finished result.
  • FoundRoll does not request microphone, location, or contacts access.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat helps FoundRoll manage products, subscription status, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process purchase status, an anonymous app user identifier, app version, and basic device information. Photo content is never sent to RevenueCat.

Tracking and advertising

FoundRoll does not use advertising trackers, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Local data and deletion

Camera preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting FoundRoll removes those local settings. App Store purchase history remains associated with your Apple Account and is controlled by Apple.

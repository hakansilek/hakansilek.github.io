FOUNDROLL CAMERA

How can we help?

FoundRoll is a photo-only vintage camera for iPhone. Here are quick answers to the most common questions.

Restore Pro

Open Settings in FoundRoll and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the iPhone uses the same Apple Account that completed the purchase.

Story and post sizes

Choose 9:16 Story, 3:4 or 4:5 Post, 1:1 Square, or 1.91:1 Landscape before taking the photo. FoundRoll keeps that selection through saving and sharing.

Camera access

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → FoundRoll. FoundRoll never requests microphone access.

Photo quality

Free exports support up to 2048 px on the longest edge. FoundRoll Pro unlocks full-resolution processing.

Import a photo

Tap the gallery button on the camera screen, choose a photo, then select a camera look. Only the photo you choose is processed.

Manage a subscription

Subscription billing is managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions to view or cancel a plan.

FOUNDROLL CAMERA

Privacy Policy

Updated October 31, 2026 · Analytics described below applies from version 2.1.4

Your photos stay on your device

FoundRoll processes photos on your iPhone. Photos you capture or choose from your library are not uploaded to FoundRoll servers. FoundRoll does not operate a photo-storage service.

Permissions

  • Camera access is used only to take photos.
  • Photos access is used only for a photo you choose and to save the finished result.
  • FoundRoll does not request microphone, location, or contacts access.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat helps FoundRoll manage products, subscription status, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process purchase status, an anonymous app user identifier, app version, and basic device information. Photo content is never sent to RevenueCat.

Usage analytics

Beginning with version 2.1.4, FoundRoll uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use the app and improve its features. We measure screen visits, filter and collage selections, aspect ratios, gallery and paywall interactions, and the outcomes of saving, sharing and purchase actions.

Google processes these events with a pseudonymous app-instance identifier and basic app and device information, such as app version, operating system, device model and language. Analytics can derive approximate location, such as a country or city, from masked IP addresses; it does not use Apple's Location Services or collect GPS coordinates. Google Analytics may also measure in-app purchase and subscription events through StoreKit, including product identifiers and prices.

We do not send your photos, filenames, photo metadata (such as EXIF), contact details or a personal account identifier to Analytics. FoundRoll does not require an account. We do not associate Analytics identifiers with a person's name, email address, RevenueCat app user identifier or advertising identifier, and do not use Analytics to identify you across other companies' apps or websites.

Advertising identifier (IDFA) collection, vendor identifier (IDFV) collection for Analytics, advertising personalization and advertising-network registration are disabled in FoundRoll's Analytics configuration. We use the collected information for product analytics, not targeted advertising.

Google provides Analytics on our behalf under its service terms. For information about its processing, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy. RevenueCat's handling of purchase information is described in its Privacy Policy.

Tracking and advertising

FoundRoll does not use advertising trackers, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Local data and deletion

Camera preferences are stored locally on your device. Deleting FoundRoll removes those local settings. App Store purchase history remains associated with your Apple Account and is controlled by Apple. Removing the app does not automatically delete analytics or purchase records already held by service providers. Those records are retained according to the applicable service settings and retention requirements. Contact us with privacy or deletion requests; because FoundRoll does not collect your name or email in analytics, we may need information that lets us locate the relevant records.

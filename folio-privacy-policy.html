Privacy Policy · Updated November 1, 2026 · Folio 1.0

Folio is an iPad note-taking app provided by Hakan Silek. This policy explains the information handled by the app and the services used for Folio Pro.

Your notebook content stays on your iPad. Folio does not upload your handwriting, text, PDFs or images to our servers or to RevenueCat. Apple handles payments; RevenueCat helps validate and restore purchases.

Notebook content and files

Folio stores notebooks, drawings, text, imported PDFs and selected images in the app’s local storage. Writing and editing work offline. Folio does not require a separate app account and does not operate its own notebook-storage server.

Optional iCloud sync (Folio Pro): If you enable iCloud sync, Folio stores notebook content, including handwriting, text, PDFs, images, infinite canvases and notebook organization, in the private app container of your own iCloud account. Apple transfers it between your iPads using iCloud Drive. We do not receive or have access to your notebook content. Sync uses your iCloud storage and follows your Apple Account settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. A local account token is used only on your iPad to pause syncing if your iCloud account changes; it is not sent to us or RevenueCat.

Sync is off until you enable it. You can turn it off in the library’s iCloud settings. Turning it off or losing Pro access stops new app sync operations and keeps local notebooks editable; it does not erase copies already stored in iCloud. Previously queued system transfers may finish. Sync keeps prior revisions to preserve concurrent edits, so storage can grow over time. You can manage or remove Folio’s iCloud data in iPad Settings after turning sync off on your devices.

If you export a PDF or a Folio backup, you choose where it goes using the iPad’s share sheet or Files app. A location such as iCloud Drive or another storage provider then follows that provider’s settings and privacy policy. Your iPad’s own backups may also include app data according to your Apple settings. Exported backups contain your notebook content, so keep them in a location you trust.

Permissions and settings

Folio uses Apple’s system pickers to access images and documents you select. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. Tool preferences and interface settings are stored locally.

Folio Pro purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Folio does not receive your payment-card details. We use RevenueCat to retrieve available products, validate purchases, manage Pro access, restore purchases and understand purchase performance.

RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription records, including product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, renewal or expiration status, store, price and currency, together with an automatically generated anonymous app user identifier and technical information such as app and operating-system versions. This information supports receipt validation, fraud prevention, purchase analytics and access to paid features.

Folio does not send your notebook content, name or email address to RevenueCat. We do not create a personal user account or link the anonymous purchase identifier to a name or email inside the app. Apple retains purchase information associated with your Apple Account under Apple’s privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Folio uses RevenueCat’s purchase analytics. It does not include a separate general-purpose usage-analytics SDK, advertising SDK or advertising identifier collection. We do not sell personal data or use your information to track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate issues. Please avoid sending private notebook contents, payment-card information or unnecessary personal details.

Retention and deletion

You can move notebooks to Folio’s Trash and restore them there. Trash changes also sync when iCloud sync is enabled. Earlier sync revisions are retained to protect concurrent edits. Removing the app can remove local notebooks and settings; make an editable Folio backup first. Removing the app does not delete iCloud content, exported copies or device backups. Manage those separately in iPad Settings or the locations where you saved them.

Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or automatically delete purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Those records may be retained to restore access, prevent fraud and meet service or legal requirements. For questions or requests about access to, correction of or deletion of information we handle, contact us below. Because the app uses an anonymous purchase identifier, we may need limited purchase information to locate the relevant record.

This website and external services

GitHub may process technical request information needed to host and protect the site under its privacy statement. Apple and RevenueCat may process information in locations outside your country under their respective policies.
